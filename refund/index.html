Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

OptHelper includes a free 7-day trial so you can try everything before you pay. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel and no renewal.

When we refund

  • Within 7 days of buying: ask and we will refund the full amount, no questions asked.
  • Charged twice, or charged but not unlocked: we will fix it or refund it, at any time.

How to ask

Email hello@buildc3.tech from the address you paid with, and include the payment ID (it starts with pay_) from Razorpay's receipt.

How long it takes

We approve refunds within 2 business days. Razorpay then returns the money to your original payment method, which usually takes 5–7 business days, depending on your bank. A refunded license is locked again.
