Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This explains what information OptHelper (the Mac app) and opthelper.buildc3.tech (this website) handle.

The app

  • On your Mac: your settings, shelf files, music library and launcher memory stay on your Mac.
  • License checks: the app creates a random device ID and keeps it, with your trial start date, in your Keychain. To know whether your Mac has paid, it sends that device ID to our website. It contains nothing that identifies you.
  • Update checks: the app downloads a small file from our website to see whether a newer version exists.
  • The AI launcher: what you type in the launcher goes to the AI provider you picked (Claude Code or Gemini), along with some context such as app names and your saved shortcuts, so it can answer. We do not receive it.

Buying

Payment is handled by Razorpay, which collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive and keep only your email address, the payment ID, the amount and currency, and the date, so we can confirm your purchase and help if you change Macs.

The website

  • Waitlist: if you join it, we keep your email address, the time, and your browser's description.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested).
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services that run the product (Razorpay for payments, Netlify for hosting), or when the law requires it.

Your choices

To see or delete the information we hold about you (your email and payment record, or your waitlist entry), email hello@buildc3.tech. Deleting a payment record means we can no longer restore your license on a new Mac.
